Privacy Policy

Your remembrance stays private.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Dhikr Pop is designed to work without collecting personal information.

Information we collect

Dhikr Pop does not collect, store, sell, rent or share personal data through the app.

The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking or third-party advertising SDKs.

Device permissions and sensitive data

Dhikr Pop does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone data or other sensitive personal information.

Dhikr activity

Your interactions with the dhikr counter and your current count remain on your device. Dhikr activity is not transmitted to our servers.

Support emails

If you choose to contact support by email, we will receive the information you voluntarily include in that message, such as your email address and the contents of your request. We use that information only to respond to you and provide support. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Children

Because the app does not require accounts or collect personal data through the app, it does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Dhikr Pop's data practices materially change in a future version, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact
