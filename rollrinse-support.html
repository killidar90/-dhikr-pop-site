Need help with RollRinse?

RollRinse helps you review photos and videos one item at a time, keep what matters, and confirm deletions before anything is removed.

Photos access

RollRinse works best with Full Photos Access. With Limited Access, the app can only review the items you selected. You can change access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → RollRinse.

Deleting media

Nothing is deleted while you swipe. Items are removed only after you review the deletion basket and confirm. Apple may keep deleted media in Photos → Recently Deleted for a period of time.

Video previews

Some videos stored in iCloud may need to download before they can be previewed. This can use mobile data depending on your device settings.
