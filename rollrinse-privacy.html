Privacy Policy

Your camera roll stays on your device.

Effective date: 26 September 2026

RollRinse is designed to help you review and clean up photos and videos locally on your Apple device.

Photos and videos

RollRinse requests access to your Photos library so you can review media, mark items for deletion, and delete selected items using Apple's Photos APIs.

RollRinse does not upload your photos or videos to a server.

Data collection

RollRinse does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking, or a backend service.

The app does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data.

Permissions

You can change RollRinse's Photos access at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Support emails

If you contact support by email, we receive the information you voluntarily include in that message and use it only to respond to your request.

Changes

If RollRinse's data practices materially change in a future version, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Al-killidar@outlook.com.
